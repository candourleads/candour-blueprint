# The Candour Blueprint — Start Here

Your AI doesn't know you. Every conversation starts from zero — so every answer comes back generic, flattering, and interchangeable with anyone else's. The Candour Blueprint fixes that. In one guided session, you'll build a persistent context document — a true mirror of who you are, how you think, how you sound, and how an AI should actually work with you. You install it once. After that, every AI conversation starts already knowing you.

This is not a personality quiz. There are no types and no labels. It is a structured, honest conversation — the kind where you end up saying the thing you've been circling for months. What gets said becomes infrastructure.

## What you need

- An account with any major AI assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. A paid plan helps (longer sessions, file uploads) but isn't required.
- Set aside 90 minutes, alone, uninterrupted — thorough runs can take up to two hours, and you can pause and resume anytime. A shorter path exists if you're pressed. Evening tends to work better than morning — you'll be more honest.
- A willingness to give real answers. The session pushes past your first answer. That's the method, and it's where the value is.

## How it works

1. **Run the session.** Open a new chat with your AI. Paste in the entire contents of `02_SessionRunner_Prompt.md`. The AI becomes your interviewer and walks you through six phases, one question at a time. Answer in your own words — voice dictation works well. You can pause and resume.
2. **Save the transcript.** When the session ends, the AI will produce a structured session record. Copy it somewhere safe.
3. **Generate your Blueprint.** Open a fresh chat. Paste in `03_Synthesis_Prompt.md`, then your session record. The AI writes your Blueprint — a master context file plus an operating protocol built from your actual answers.
4. **Install it.** Follow `04_Install_Guides.md` for your platform. Five minutes.
5. **Keep it alive.** `05_Keep_It_Alive.md` is the quarterly review ritual. A blueprint that never updates becomes a costume. Fifteen minutes, four times a year.

## Two things before you start

**On honesty.** The session works at the depth you bring to it. Some questions go near uncomfortable territory — the gap between how you present and how you actually are. You can skip anything. But the questions you most want to skip are usually the ones doing the most work. This is a thinking exercise, not therapy — if what comes up needs more than thinking, take it to a professional who can hold it properly.

**On privacy.** Your answers stay in your own AI account. Nothing comes back to us — no data, no transcript, no analytics. The Blueprint contains real material about you; install the full version only on accounts you control, and use the trimmed version (the session will offer one) for shared or work accounts.

When you're ready, open `02_SessionRunner_Prompt.md`, copy all of it, and paste it into a new chat.
