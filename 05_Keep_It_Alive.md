# Keep It Alive

A blueprint that never updates becomes a costume — a description of who you were the day you wrote it, worn long after it stopped fitting. The review takes fifteen minutes, four times a year. Put it in the calendar now; quarterly, any date you'll actually keep.

## The quarterly review

Open a chat with your AI (with the Blueprint installed) and paste this:

> It's time for my quarterly Blueprint review. Walk me through it one question at a time:
> 1. Read back my Section 8 (current context). What's stale? I'll give you this quarter's reality — live projects, pressures, open decisions.
> 2. Did my circumstances change in any way that touches Sections 1 or 4 — new role, new venture, something shipped, something ended?
> 3. Based on our conversations this quarter: where did you challenge me and it worked? Where did the protocol misfire — too blunt, too soft, wrong moment? Propose specific edits to Section 5.
> 4. Did any writing I produced this quarter drift from Section 3? Any new banned words to add, any new sample worth capturing?
> 5. Is anything in this Blueprint no longer true? Truth decays quietly — check the failure modes especially. If I've genuinely outgrown one, it comes out.
> When we're done, produce the updated Blueprint in full, bump the version number, and add one line to the change log saying what changed and why.

Then reinstall the updated file everywhere it lives. An updated blueprint in one tool and a stale one in another is worse than either alone.

## Between reviews

Two habits, both small:

- **When the AI misses, fix the document.** If it flatters you, challenges you wrongly, or writes something that doesn't sound like you — don't just correct the conversation. Ask it: "What line would I add to my Blueprint so you never do that again?" Add the line.
- **When you say something true out loud for the first time** — in a session, a conversation, a draft — capture it. The best lines in your Blueprint will be the ones you earned after you wrote it.

## When to redo the session entirely

The quarterly review maintains; it doesn't rebuild. Run the full session again (02_SessionRunner_Prompt.md) when something structural changes: a new venture, a role change, a relocation, the end of something that defined Section 4. Expect that to be every couple of years, not every quarter. Keep the old Blueprint when you do — the distance between versions is a record of who you're becoming, and it's worth having.
